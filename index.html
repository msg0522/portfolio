By Maria Goretti Steve, staff designer

Maria Goretti Steve is a multimedia designer who gives small businesses a name, a look and a way to talk.

She has named a bakery, steered a yearly pop-up sale through two identities, and has printed patterns onto fabric since her textile days at NIFT and FIT.

She recently finished a Master's in Management of Technology at NYU, which explains the gown.

Asked what she needs to get started, she said: "A brief, and coffee."
