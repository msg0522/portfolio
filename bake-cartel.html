Case study · Naming and brand identity

Home baker gets a name. And a face.

A home bakery in Kottayam had sweet and savoury on the menu and no brand of its own. I named it, designed its identity and make its static posts.

Since 2022Kottayam, KeralaNaming · Identity · Social
primary logo

01 · The brief

No name. No logo. A whole menu.

A new home bakery was launching on Instagram with tarts, cheesecakes, puff pastry, cakes and savoury bakes.

It had no logo of its own, only a Canva stock image.

It needed a brand that felt clean, warm and appetising. Not loud.

Three batches. One keeper.

I explored a baker character, an illustrated cake wordmark, and marks built from the tools of the trade: hands, bowls, cups and spoons.

04 · The decision

Chefs measure everything. Sweet or savoury.

Measuring spoons cover the whole menu and the care behind it, sweet or savoury.

Muted, dough-toned colours read appetising and quiet, matched to the owner's taste.

A clean typeface balances the illustrated mark, now rolled out to business cards.
